Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Clear details about privacy, availability and current features.

Are my files uploaded?

The current tools are designed to process inputs in your browser. ToolNest does not receive tool inputs through its own application backend.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the currently published core tools.

Are ads and affiliate links active?

No. Ad placements are reserved and affiliate links are not active in this initial release.

Can I pay for Premium?

Not yet. Checkout and paid access are disabled until a secure external payment and entitlement flow is configured.

Will every image become smaller?

No. Results depend on image content, output format and quality. The tool reports when an output is larger.

Can I use results for critical decisions?

Always verify important calculations, encoded values and exported files before relying on them.
